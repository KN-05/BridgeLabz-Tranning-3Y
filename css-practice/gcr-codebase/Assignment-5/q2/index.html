Overview

Manali is a beautiful hill town in Himachal Pradesh, India, known for its snow-capped mountains, rivers, and pleasant weather.

Places to Visit

Popular spots include Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass, Hadimba Temple and the Old Manali market area.

Local Food

Try the local Himachali Dham, Siddu, and hot momos while enjoying the cool mountain air.

Travel Tips

Carry warm clothes, book stays in advance during peak season, and always check the weather before heading to high altitude areas.
